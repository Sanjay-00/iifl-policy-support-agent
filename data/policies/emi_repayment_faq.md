## EMI Due Dates

EMIs are due on the 5th, 10th or 15th of each month, as chosen at the time of disbursal. The first EMI date is shown in the welcome letter. The due date can be changed once during the loan tenure. Interest for the broken period between the old and new due date is added to the next EMI.

## Payment Modes

EMIs are collected through a NACH auto-debit mandate on the registered bank account. Customers can also pay an EMI manually through UPI or net banking on the customer portal. A manual payment must be made at least 2 working days before the due date so that the auto-debit is not presented.

## Changing the Repayment Bank Account

To move EMI auto-debit to a different bank account, submit a new NACH mandate through the customer portal or at a branch. Registration of a new mandate can take up to 30 days. Until the new mandate is confirmed by SMS, EMIs continue to be debited from the existing account, so it should not be closed. A mandate swap charge of Rs. 500 applies.

## EMI Bounce and Penal Charges

If an EMI is not paid on the due date because of insufficient balance or a stopped mandate, a bounce charge of Rs. 750 plus GST is levied for each bounced instrument. In addition, a penal charge of 2% per month is levied on the overdue EMI amount for the number of days it remains unpaid. Penal charges are not added to the interest rate and no interest is charged on them.

## Reminders and Grace Period

There is no grace period after the EMI due date. A reminder SMS is sent 3 days before each due date. Overdue accounts are reported to credit bureaus in the regular monthly reporting cycle.

## Moratorium and Financial Hardship

No EMI moratorium or payment holiday is offered as a standard facility. Customers facing financial hardship, such as loss of income or a medical emergency, should contact customer care before the due date. Each request is reviewed individually by the collections team, and any relief depends on that review.

## Repayment Schedule and Statements

The repayment schedule, statement of account and interest certificate can be downloaded free of charge from the customer portal. A physical copy of the statement of account is free once per financial year. Each additional physical copy costs Rs. 250.
