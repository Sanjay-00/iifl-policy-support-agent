> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for a take-home exercise. It is not an official IIFL Finance policy.

## Scope

This policy applies to personal loans, business loans and home loans. It explains how a borrower can repay a loan fully (foreclosure or pre-closure) or partially (part-prepayment) before the scheduled maturity date. Gold loans are covered by a separate policy.

## Eligibility for Foreclosure

A loan can be foreclosed after the borrower has paid at least 6 EMIs. Floating-rate loans taken by individuals for non-business purposes have no lock-in period and can be foreclosed at any time.

## Foreclosure Charges

- Floating-rate loans taken by individuals for non-business purposes: nil.
- Fixed-rate personal loans: 4% of the principal outstanding, plus GST.
- Business loans: 5% of the principal outstanding, plus GST.

The foreclosure charge is calculated on the principal outstanding on the date of payment, not on the original loan amount.

## Part-Prepayment

Customers may prepay part of the outstanding principal before the scheduled maturity date. The minimum part-prepayment is an amount equal to 1 EMI. Up to 25% of the principal outstanding can be prepaid in a financial year without any charge. A charge of 2% applies to the amount prepaid above that limit.

After a part-prepayment the customer can choose to reduce either the EMI or the remaining tenure. If no choice is made, the tenure is reduced and the EMI stays the same.

## Foreclosure Process

1. Request a foreclosure statement through the customer portal, the mobile app or any branch.
2. The foreclosure statement is issued within 3 working days and is valid for 7 calendar days.
3. Pay the amount shown on the statement by NEFT, RTGS or demand draft. Cash payments above Rs. 2,00,000 are not accepted.

The foreclosure amount includes principal outstanding, interest accrued up to the payment date, foreclosure charges and any unpaid penal charges.

## Closure Documents and NOC

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued within 15 working days after the full amount is received and cleared. The NACH mandate is cancelled within 5 working days of closure. For secured loans, original property documents are returned within 30 days of closure.

## Refund of Excess Amount

If the borrower pays more than the foreclosure amount, the excess is refunded to the registered bank account within 10 working days of closure.
