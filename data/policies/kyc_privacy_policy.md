> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for a take-home exercise. It is not an official IIFL Finance policy.

## Accepted KYC Documents

PAN is mandatory for every borrower. For proof of identity and address, any one of the following is accepted: passport, driving licence, voter ID card or Aadhaar. When an Aadhaar copy is submitted, the first 8 digits must be masked.

## Updating Address or Mobile Number

To update the address, upload a valid address proof on the customer portal or submit it at a branch. Address updates are processed within 7 working days. A mobile number can be updated on the portal after OTP verification on both the old and the new number. If the old number is no longer available, the update must be done at a branch with original identity proof.

## Periodic KYC Update

KYC details must be refreshed periodically: every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers. Customers are notified by SMS and email when their KYC update is due.

## How Customer Data Is Used and Shared

Customer data is used to process the loan, service the account and meet legal obligations. It is shared only with credit bureaus, regulators and law enforcement when required by law, and with service providers such as collection agencies bound by confidentiality agreements. Customer data is never sold. Marketing messages are sent only with the customer's consent, which can be withdrawn at any time from the customer portal.

## Data Retention

Loan and KYC records are retained for 5 years after the loan is closed, or longer if required by law. After this period the records are deleted or anonymised.

## Fraud Prevention

Staff will never ask for an OTP, PIN, CVV or password by phone, SMS or email. Customers who receive such a request, or who notice an unauthorised transaction, should report it to customer care within 3 days.

## Grievance Redressal

Complaints can be raised through customer care, email or any branch, and a response is given within 7 working days. If the complaint is not resolved within 15 days, the customer can escalate it to the Grievance Redressal Officer, whose contact details are published on the website.
